The three gaps

Inventory — no report is possible yet
Not an oversight in the design: there is no stock ledger. batchdetail.Quantity is a hand-edited VARCHAR that no sale ever decrements, so any “stock on hand” number would be invented. It stays out of Reports until stock movements are recorded.
Feedback — the data is there, the report is not
pos_feedback is CRUD only. Ratings exist, and one rating per visit is enforced, but nothing aggregates them: no average over time, no rating by dish, no tie to the return reasons that mark our own fault. It is one query away and would answer a question nothing else in the system answers.
Token queue — built, reachable by nobody
/api/pos/tokens/stats works and the Channels tab fetches it, but only to decorate that tab. Counter throughput and wait times have no screen of their own.
